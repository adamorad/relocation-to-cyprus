## Notes / Uncertainties

1. **`retiring-in-cyprus` — pension 5% election break-even**: The 2026 band changes materially alter the progressive vs flat-rate comparison. The old guidance said "most retirees find the flat rate better" (true under old bands where progressive started at €0). Under 2026 bands, the flat rate is only better above ~€28,000/year pension income. I updated the example and noted the break-even; the election mechanics (made annually, irrevocable for the year) were left unchanged as those are administrative, not tax-rate facts.

2. **SDC on rental — non-dom wording in `digital-nomad-visa-guide`**: The original text said non-dom "exempts you entirely from SDC on … rental income." Since SDC on rental is abolished for everyone, saying non-dom "exempts" you from it is now moot. I updated to say non-dom exempts from SDC on dividends and interest, with a parenthetical noting the rental abolition. If the editorial preference is to drop the SDC-rental mention entirely rather than use a parenthetical, that would also be defensible.
